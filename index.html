About Me

DT

I am a 12-year-old developer who started with one simple question: what happens if I change this line? That curiosity turned into hands-on practice. Ideas I sketched on paper became structured HTML, styled with CSS, and brought to life with JavaScript.

Technical precision is my foundation. I write clean HTML, style with CSS, script in standard JavaScript, automate repetitive work with Bash, and deploy projects to Render and Netlify. Each project teaches me something new, and each one makes me want to build the next.

Creativity keeps my work balanced. Drawing and sketching give me an eye for visual balance and composition, while singing teaches me rhythm and timing, which I carry into how I think about motion and pacing in an interface.

Off the screen, I play football. It builds teamwork, adaptability, and high energy, and it teaches me to read the game a few steps ahead, a habit that shows up when I plan and debug code.
